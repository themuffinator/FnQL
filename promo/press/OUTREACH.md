# Creator and community outreach

Send in Phase 2, after the launch posts have collected some feedback. Keep
each message personal: mention something specific from the recipient's
channel or community. Don't mass-mail.

## Email / DM template

**Subject:** FnQL: open-source engine for Quake Live (joins retail servers)

> Hi <name>,
>
> I enjoyed your <specific video/stream/post>. I'm the developer of
> **FnQL**, an open-source replacement engine for the Steam release of Quake
> Live, and I thought it might interest you and your audience.
>
> In short: it keeps retail compatibility (FnQL players join retail servers,
> retail players join FnQL servers, demos and Workshop content keep working)
> and adds GLx and Vulkan renderers with HDR, raw input that uses Quake
> Live's own mouse math, HRTF audio and a modern console. The latest
> release also runs our own open-source game modules by default.
>
> - Download: https://github.com/themuffinator/FnQL/releases/latest
> - Screenshots, logo and fact sheet: <press kit link>
> - Source: https://github.com/themuffinator/FnQL
>
> No obligation at all. If you try it, I'd love to hear what breaks; if you
> want to cover it, I'm happy to answer questions or jump on a call.
>
> Thanks,
> <name>
>
> *FnQL is an independent project, not affiliated with id Software,
> Bethesda, ZeniMax or Valve.*
